## How to use

Copy the English layout, it should wind up looking like:

Languages/YourLanguage/DefInjected
Languages/YourLanguage/Keyed

Keyed contains one file, Vampires.xml. DefInjected should have a whole bunch of individual folders with CallAVampire.xml inside them.
Once you've copied them over, you'll have to open up and replace the English text inside the XML keys

As an example:

  <ConsumeHemogenBelow>Consume blood below</ConsumeHemogenBelow>

might be replaced with

  <ConsumeHemogenBelow>Konsum bei Blut unter</ConsumeHemogenBelow>

Don't edit things in square brackets [like this], curly braces {like this}, or symbols that look like either of these: "->" "\n" 

As far as I know, the translations will work if you just publish it as a mod with an About file, but I have no complaints about putting them directly into the official release.
